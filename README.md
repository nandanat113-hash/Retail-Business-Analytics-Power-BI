# Retail-Business-Analytics-Power-BI
Interactive Retail Business Analytics dashboard built using Power BI to analyze sales, profit, customers, products, regions, and business performance.
## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-selling and profitable products
- Analyze sales by category and segment
- Compare current year sales with previous year
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Identify valuable customers
- Analyze regional sales and profit
- Monitor key business KPIs

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Analysis

Key insights include:

- Total Sales
- Total Sales LY
- YoY Sales Growth
- Average Sales per Order
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Segment
- Top Selling Products
- Sales Comparison
- Sales by Country

### 2. Profit Analysis

Key insights include:

- Total Profit
- Total Profit LY
- Profit Growth
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Segment
- Top Profitable Products
- Monthly Profit Comparison
- Profit Performance

### 3. Customer & Demographic Analysis

Key insights include:

- Total Customers
- Total Orders
- Total Products
- Top Active Buyers
- Top Valuable Customers
- Top Profitable States
- Profit by Order Priority
- Sales and Profit by Region
